CharityGraph public data commitments

CharityGraph is a provenance-preserving public data layer for Australian charities. It describes and structures public information; it does not rate or recommend charities.

  • Published identities are durable; historical releases remain interpretable and new observations append rather than overwrite history.
  • Direct observations, deterministic transformations, LLM interpretations and estimates are identified separately; missing information is not a negative finding.
  • Organisations and others can inspect evidence and methods and contest errors through governed correction, privacy, safety, abuse and legal processes.
  • CharityGraph does not accept payment for inclusion, exclusion, ranking, favourable editorial treatment or removal of adequately supported information. Material funding relationships and editorial conflicts will be disclosed.
  • CharityGraph never publishes charity rankings, recommendations, reputation scores, mandate-fit scores or fundraising-efficiency judgements. Downstream adaptations are governed by their publishers and must not imply official status.
  • Commercial reuse remains permitted under the applicable licence with attribution and upstream-rights obligations.

This is an early 120-subject test release. It does not represent national enriched coverage.
